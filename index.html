Road Spotter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Road Spotter is a car-spotting game designed for passengers. This privacy policy explains how the current release handles information.

Information and game progress

The current release stores game progress locally on your device. This can include journey scores, spotted vehicle counts, journey history and game status.

No account is required to use the current release.

Personal data

Based on the current release, Road Spotter does not collect or transmit precise or approximate location, camera data, contacts, personal identity information or payment information.

Sharing

The current release does not send locally stored game progress to the developer or share that local game progress with third parties.

Deleting local data

Journey history can be cleared within the app where that option is available. Uninstalling Road Spotter or clearing the app's data through your device settings can also remove locally stored app data.

Safety

Road Spotter is designed for passengers. Drivers should never use a phone or play Road Spotter while driving.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact email shown on Road Spotter's Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Road Spotter's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
